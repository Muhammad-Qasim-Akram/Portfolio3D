The problem

What it had to do.

Chat looks simple until two people are connected to different server processes. These four constraints shaped every decision below.

01

Instant, not polled

A message has to appear on every screen in the room without anyone refreshing or the client hammering the API.

02

Authenticated sockets

REST uses JWT. The WebSocket has to trust the same identity, and browsers can't attach headers to one.

03

More than one worker

Two users in the same room may be held by different processes. They still have to hear each other.

04

One-person deployment

Frontend on Vercel, backend, Postgres and Redis on Railway. Few moving parts, easy to redeploy.
