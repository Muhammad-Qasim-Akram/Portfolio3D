The problem

It looked great and ran badly.

Screen recording the site made it stutter, and scrolling past project cards made them shake. Four rules shaped the fix.

01

Keep the look

Glass, glow and tilt are the point of the site. Cheaper paths, not fewer effects.

02

Measure first

Rank the costs before touching code, so every change has a reason.

03

No build step

Plain HTML, CSS and JS on Netlify. Fixes have to work without tooling.

04

Phones count

Same effects on touch, with the same interactions and a lower cost.
